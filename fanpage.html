About V

Kim Taehyung, known on stage as V, is a South Korean singer, songwriter, and member of the boy band BTS. Since debuting in 2013, he's built a reputation for a deep, husky voice, a painter's eye for style, and a habit of turning up wherever art, fashion, and film collide. Off stage he's an avid photographer and self-taught painter, often folding those interests straight into his music.
